Cascade Commerce Bank is offering a bonus of up to $3,000 when you open a new Business Advantage Checking account and make and maintain new money deposits. You must deposit funds within 30 days of account opening and maintain the balance from days 31 through day 90.

  • $500 bonus when you deposit $10,000
  • $900 bonus when you deposit $25,000
  • $1,500 bonus when you deposit $75,000
  • $3,000 bonus when you deposit $250,000

After you satisfy all of the requirements, the bonus will be deposited into your new account within 60 days. This offer is intended for new customers only; you are not eligible if you were an owner or signer on a Cascade Commerce Business Checking account within the last twelve (12) months.

Fee is waived for smaller companies for the first 12 months, otherwise you must:

  • Maintain a $5,000 combined average balance, or
  • Spend at least $500 in new net purchases on your business debit card, or
  • Become a member of Preferred Rewards for Business
