Calendar picker — preview

This is a standalone mock of the color-coded date picker for choosing when to initiate a direct deposit. Each business day is shaded by the total round-trip hold that results if you initiate that day — green = shortest, red = longest (a Friday or pre-holiday drags the weekend/holiday into the round trip). Weekends & US federal holidays are non-initiable (grey, holiday = red dot). Uses the same business-day + 1/1/1 "season" model as the app. Nothing here touches the real app — it's a visual to react to.

In the real app this replaces the native date input on every date field. For held / new-funds offers it would shade by the funding-date weekend/holiday shift instead of the DD round trip; for withdrawal dates, by when funds actually release. The “best/worst” coloring is always relative to the minimum possible hold in view.
